🌌 The Republic of Luminara

"Ex Tenebris, Lux" (Out of Darkness, Light)

☀️ About Our Nation

Founded on 28 October 2026, the Republic of Luminara is a sovereign online entity. Built on the core values of environmental sustainability, light, and innovation, our citizens connect globally to forge a green, forward-thinking community breaking through physical borders.

🇳🇫 National Symbols

Our national flag features a vibrant electric blue and golden yellow layout set against a dark black field. This design represents the striking radiance of clean energy and intellect piercing through darkness.

🪙 The ASP Monetary System

Luminara operates on a custom, non-decimal financial system backed directly by the Solar Standard (tracked green energy credits matching real-world power tracking):

  • A – Aurora: The premier reserve unit (Worth 9 Solars)
  • S – Solar: The core currency unit (Worth 16 Phots)
  • P – Phot: The base fractional unit of light

System Mathematics: 16 Phots make 1 Solar | 9 Solars make 1 Aurora (144 Phots total).
